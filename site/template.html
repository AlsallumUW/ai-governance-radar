Scope, evidence & limitations

This edition covers 193 UN Member States, the State of Palestine and the Holy See. Individual discovery and focused procurement, security and regulator searches are recorded for each. These searches are a research starting point; they do not replace the full 14-pass national review.

LabelMeaning
Reviewed metadataThe populated factual fields were reviewed against identified official sources. Null fields remain unknown; this does not mean the entire jurisdiction is reviewed.
Source locatedA primary official publication or page was located. Authority, dates, legal effect, applicability and version history may still need review.
Verified presentAt least one primary source record exists for the category. Depth and exhaustive coverage remain unfinished.
Not yet fully reviewedThe category is incomplete. Empty results are not evidence that a government has published nothing.

EU instruments are stored once and inherited by the 27 Member States. A regional record’s presence is not a claim that every provision governs every AI use. Voluntary codes, administrative policies, guidance, drafts and binding regulations retain different labels. Other regional layers are browsable; national adoption and treaty applicability are not assumed.

Only original government or official-body documents and substantive publication pages appear in the catalogue. News and announcement-only pages are excluded. Link checks distinguish reachable, redirected, inaccessible and broken URLs; anti-bot responses are not labelled working. Source locations and link-check dates are auditable in the exports.

No rankings or automatic update/monitoring features are included. Historical relationships, sectoral depth and remaining national categories require further document review.
