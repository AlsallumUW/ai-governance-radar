About this radar

The AI Governance Radar records the official documents through which governments govern artificial intelligence. It covers the 193 UN Member States, the State of Palestine and the Holy See, and regional bodies such as the EU and the GCC.

Only official sources are used: official gazettes and legal portals, and the websites of ministries, regulators and courts. News reports are never used.

What the labels mean

  • Binding or mandatory: laws, regulations, treaties and policies that must be followed within their scope.
  • Non-binding: strategies, guidelines, codes and opinions.
  • Drafts: texts published for consultation and not yet adopted.
  • Leads: documents found but not yet verified; they are not counted.

Changes are checked daily for one group of countries at a time, and the whole dataset is reviewed weekly.

Counts show how many official documents were found. They do not measure the quality or strength of a country's AI governance.

Limits: some official websites cannot be reached, and the absence of a record does not mean the absence of a policy. Instruments of regional and international bodies are kept on each body's own page and are not counted for member states.
